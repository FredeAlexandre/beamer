the encoder (hooks: `streams.{h,cpp}` 4 lines, `helper.cpp` tick 9 lines, `sources.txt`). Detection on the mono
mix: 1024-pt Hann FFT every 512 samples; a line (peak +-2 bins, 330 Hz-16 kHz) holding >= 40 % of the band, no
partial at f/2/2f/3f and no other line within 15 dB, whose level rises >= 6 dB at a stable frequency over 0.26,

1 s once the tone fell 12 dB; a tone that never decays under the duck is the source's own: released after 4 s,

freq_hz, duck_db}` per change. New dev test spec `rise:<hz>[:<dB/s>]`. Python: `howl` -> `snapshot.howls`

**Deviations.** Notice wording: speakers are on T (receiver), the mic on M (sender), so the advice is
"headphones on T", not on the mic's machine as in the brief's template. No deeper duck: a loop gain > 20 dB is not
plausible here and "still rising under the duck" mostly meant source content. Tones below 330 Hz are not
watched (47 Hz bins cannot tell a chord or voice from one line). Known limits: a pure tone fading in slowly gets
one 4 s duck; a loop with gain > 1 over ~1 kHz at once (many lines) is not caught in simulation; the receiver
sees the howl only while its page watches. it.po gained ~120 untranslated audio strings it lacked (may conflict
with other WPs regenerating it).
